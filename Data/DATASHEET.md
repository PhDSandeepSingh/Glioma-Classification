## 1. Motivation

**Why was the dataset created?**
- The UCI "Glioma Grading Clinical and Mutation Features" dataset (UCI ID 759; doi:10.24432/C5R62J) is used as a machine-learning benchmark.
- Its target is the legacy TCGA project label: lower-grade glioma (LGG) versus glioblastoma (GBM), a histological diagnosis made before the 2021 WHO classification.
- Our analysis showed that this label disagrees with IDH status in 13.1% of patients, and that almost all model errors fall in those patients.
- This file lets other users of the benchmark:
  1. see, for each patient, how the legacy label relates to IDH status and to approximate WHO 2021 categories;
  2. evaluate models against those categories, or stratify by label–IDH concordance;
  3. reproduce the counts in the article from one file.

**Who created it, and on whose behalf?**
- Created by the authors of the article (Sandeep Singh, Ashok Pal, Nitin Mittal, Jatinder Kaur, Fikreselam Gared) as part of revising "Diagnostic label–IDH discordance and machine-learning classifier performance on a legacy TCGA glioma grading benchmark" for *Scientific Reports*.
- The release was requested by the peer reviewers (Reviewer 3, M2; editorial item RR-9).

**Funding.** None (see the article's Funding statement).

## 2. Composition

**What do the instances represent?**
- Each row is one TCGA patient with diffuse glioma in the UCI analysis file: 839 patients, 487 legacy LGG and 352 legacy GBM.
- The file holds no images, sequence data or free text written by clinicians, apart from the raw primary-diagnosis category.

**Is this all possible instances or a sample?**
- It covers every patient in the UCI analysis file.
- The analysis file is itself a complete-case subset of the raw UCI/TCGA export: 23 of 862 patients were excluded there for missing sex, age or race.
- The TCGA cohort is a research biobank, not a population sample. It comes mainly from North American tissue source sites, and 91.2% of patients are White.

**Rule variants** (Section 20j of the notebook):

| Variant | Rule |
|---|---|
| `base` | R3 rules; IDH status from the dataset's IDH1/IDH2 calls |
| `b` | Ceccarelli curated IDH status (dataset calls as fallback) |
| `c` | Molecular glioblastoma criteria applied only to astrocytic legacy histology; IDH-wildtype oligodendroglial or mixed tumours are coded "NEC (unassignable)" |
| `d` | IDH-wildtype tumours meeting no criterion are coded "NEC (unassignable)" and excluded from both targets |
| `e` | `b` + `c` + `d` |
| `f` | `base` + H3 status |
| `g` | `b` + `c` + `d` + H3 (most conservative) |

Variants `d2`, `h1` and `h0` appear in the article's sensitivity tables but are not columns of the release.

**Is there a label or target?**
- The benchmark target is `Legacy_label`.
- The `WHO2021_category_*` and `WHO_*` columns are *derived, approximate* targets. They are not independent ground truth (see "Known limitations").

**Is any information missing?**
- **TERT promoter status:** missing for 529 of 839 patients (319 of 352 legacy GBM, 210 of 487 legacy LGG). IDH-wildtype legacy LGG with no detected criterion and unknown TERT are "not assessable", and their targets are blank.
- **Curated IDH status:** `IDH_status_Ceccarelli` is blank for 7 patients; variant `b` then falls back to the dataset's IDH1/IDH2 calls.
- **Copy number:** `EGFR_GISTIC`, `CDKN2A_GISTIC`, `CDKN2B_GISTIC` and `Chr7gain_Chr10loss` are blank for the same 19 patients (no copy-number profile).
- **Methylation cluster:** blank for 65 patients.
- **1p/19q status:** available for 828 of 839.
- **Exome-based fields:** H3 and the IDH hotspot are available only for the 764 patients in the study's list of exome-sequenced samples. The other 75 read "Not exome-sequenced", which is *not* the same as wild-type.
- **Not available at all:** histological features (necrosis, microvascular proliferation), tumour location (needed for diffuse midline glioma), methylation-class calls, and diagnosis dates.

**Relationships between instances.**
- Each row is a distinct patient.
- Two patients (TCGA-P5-A72W and TCGA-E1-A7Z4) have identical rows in the UCI analysis file because their ages round to the same value; their `Case_ID`s differ.

**Recommended splits.** `Partition` reproduces the article's train/test split. Users who compare against the article should keep it.

**Errors, noise or redundancy**
- The dataset's IDH1/2 calls and Ceccarelli's curated IDH status disagree for 7 of the 832 patients with both; variant `b` uses the curated status.
- GISTIC calls approximate high-level amplification and homozygous deletion.
- The legacy label itself is the subject of the study: it is a histological diagnosis of the WHO 2007 era.

**Self-contained?**
- The file is self-contained for the fields listed.
- Demographic predictors and the 20 mutation flags are *not* duplicated. Join them from the UCI analysis file with `UCI_row_index`, or from the raw export with `Case_ID`.
- The external inputs are pinned by commit and SHA-256 in `Data/External/README.md`.

**Confidential or sensitive data?**
- TCGA open-access data only: de-identified barcodes and somatic, tumour-level calls. No germline data, no names or dates.
- `Primary_Diagnosis_raw` and the predictions concern a serious disease. The file contains no information that identifies a person beyond public TCGA barcodes.

## 3. Collection process

**How was the data acquired?** All inputs are existing public data; nothing was collected from patients for this study.
1. **UCI dataset 759** (CC BY 4.0). The 839-patient analysis file and the 862-patient raw TCGA export with case IDs, project and primary diagnosis. SHA-256 values are in `README_R3.md` (Data provenance) and the compliance note `data_provenance.md`.
2. **cBioPortal study `lgggbm_tcga_pub`** (Ceccarelli et al., *Cell* 2016), datahub commit `18763f8eee86d4886d7210068f745f1e509498ee`, downloaded 12 October 2026. From it we took:
   - `data_clinical_sample.txt` (IDH status, 1p/19q, +7/−10, TERT, methylation cluster);
   - `data_cna.txt` (GISTIC calls for EGFR, CDKN2A, CDKN2B);
   - `data_mutations.txt` (IDH and H3 records, sequenced-sample list).

**Linkage.**
- Every released UCI row was mapped to a raw-export row by reproducing the released file from the raw export: exclusion of missing sex, age or race, and conversion of age to years plus days/365, rounded to 2 decimals. This mapping reproduced all 24 fields of every row and gives each patient a TCGA case ID.
- External annotations were joined on the 12-character TCGA patient barcode (`Case_ID` = `PATIENT_ID`).

**Who was involved?** The authors, using code. There was no manual labelling.

**Time frame.** Inputs were obtained in 2022–2026 (UCI release December 2022; cBioPortal snapshot October 2026). TCGA specimens were collected earlier; their dates are not in the source files.

**Ethical review.** Not required: secondary analysis of public, de-identified data. TCGA obtained consent and ethics approval at the contributing sites.

## 4. Preprocessing, cleaning and labelling

**Reconstruction rules (base variant).** These are the rules of `who2021()` and `who2021_v()` in `build_notebook.py`.

*IDH-mutant tumours:*
1. 1p/19q-codeleted → **oligodendroglioma, IDH-mutant, 1p/19q-codeleted** (grade-4 target 0).
2. 1p/19q unknown → "IDH-mutant, 1p/19q unknown"; grade 4 unassignable.
3. Non-codeleted, with legacy GBM histology (proxy for necrosis or microvascular proliferation) **or** a CDKN2A/B deep deletion (GISTIC −2) → **astrocytoma, IDH-mutant, CNS WHO grade 4**.
4. Non-codeleted, CDKN2A call unavailable → grade not assignable.
5. Otherwise → **astrocytoma, IDH-mutant, grade 2–3**.

*IDH-wildtype tumours:*
1. Legacy GBM → **glioblastoma, IDH-wildtype (histological criteria)**.
2. Otherwise, TERT promoter mutation, EGFR high-level amplification (GISTIC 2) or combined +7/−10 → **glioblastoma, IDH-wildtype (molecular criteria)**.
3. Otherwise, with TERT wild-type and EGFR and +7/−10 known → "glioblastoma criteria not met" (targets 0).
4. Otherwise → "not assessable"; targets blank.

*Derived targets:*
- `WHO_grade4_*` = CNS WHO grade 4 (1/0).
- `WHO_GBM_IDHwt_*` = glioblastoma, IDH-wildtype (1/0).
- Blank means the target could not be assigned. Such patients are excluded from that target in the article.

**H3 (variants `f` and `g`).**
- Only K27M (H3.3 or H3.1) and G34R/V (H3.3) hotspots are used. MAF numbering counts the initiator methionine, so p.K28M is K27M and p.G35R/V is G34R/V.
- An IDH-wildtype G34-mutant tumour → diffuse hemispheric glioma, H3 G34-mutant (grade 4; not glioblastoma).
- An IDH-wildtype K27M tumour → "Diffuse glioma, H3 K27M-mutant (location unknown)"; grade unassignable, because the tumour's midline location cannot be checked.

**Model outputs.**
- Training patients: out-of-fold predictions from nested cross-validation.
- Test patients: the locked final model, scored once.
- All probabilities are Platt-calibrated. The threshold of 0.351 was locked on the training partition.

**When the rules were fixed.** The rules were fixed **after** the test set had been scored (disclosed in the article). They were not used for any model fitting, selection or locked decision.

**Is the raw data available?** Yes. The UCI files are in `Data/`; the external subsets and their derivation are in `Data/External/`.

**Software.** Python 3.13.16; package versions in `requirements_r3.txt` and `environment/requirements-lock.txt`.

## 5. Uses

**Intended uses**
- Re-scoring models trained on the UCI/TCGA glioma benchmark against approximate WHO 2021 tumour-type and grade targets.
- Reporting accuracy separately for label–IDH concordant and discordant patients.
- Comparing any model with the no-fit IDH1/2 rule.
- Studying label noise that depends on features, and benchmark validity.
- Reproducing the article's tables.

**Uses to avoid. Do not use this file:**
- **For clinical diagnosis, grading, prognosis or treatment decisions** for any individual. The categories are *approximate* WHO 2021 categories inferred from research annotations, without histopathological review, methylation profiling or tumour location.
- **As ground truth that validates a model.** The `GBM_IDHwt` target is defined partly by IDH status, which is also a model input. Agreement between a model and that target is therefore partly true by construction.
- **To estimate population prevalence of WHO 2021 entities.** TCGA is a selected research cohort, and two separately accrued projects are combined.
- **To evaluate algorithms that take** `Primary_Diagnosis_raw`, `Project` or any derived category **as a predictor.** These fields encode the target and would leak it.
- **For subgroup or fairness conclusions about non-White patients.** Only 59 Black, 14 Asian and 1 American Indian or Alaska Native patients are included.

**Anything else users should know?**
- Results differ by rule variant. Always report which variant (`base`, `b` … `g`) was used.
- The 7 patients whose IDH sources disagree, and the not-assessable IDH-wildtype LGG, are the main sources of variation between variants.
